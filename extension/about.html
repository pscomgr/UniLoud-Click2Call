UniLoud Click-to-Call

Place calls from Chrome through your own FreePBX or Asterisk server.

How it works

  1. Your administrator installs the PHP 8.x backend on your PBX.
  2. You enter the PBX HTTPS URL and your personal API credentials.
  3. The extension asks the PBX to ring your phone, then connects the destination.

There is no UniLoud cloud relay. Call details go only to the PBX URL you configure.

Privacy choices

  • Automatic page phone detection is optional and requires a separate permission.
  • Page URL context is off by default and removes query strings, fragments and credentials.
  • Your API secret is restricted to trusted extension pages; you can keep it session-only.

First setup

Open the extension popup, expand Connection settings, connect to your PBX and select your extension.
